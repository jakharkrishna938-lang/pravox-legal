Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026

Introduction

PRAVOX respects user privacy. This Privacy Policy describes what information PRAVOX collects, uses, and stores when users use the application.

1. Information We Collect

When users create a PRAVOX account using email/password authentication, PRAVOX may process and store information associated with the account, including the Firebase user ID, email address, display name, profile photo URL if available, and profile/account settings and state.

2. Music and Library Data

PRAVOX stores user-specific app activity required to provide personal library features, including Liked Songs, Favourite Songs, Playlists, Recently Played, and Continue Listening information. This information is associated with the authenticated PRAVOX account.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to authenticate users, maintain PRAVOX accounts, provide profile functionality, save personal music-library preferences, provide Likes, Favourites and Playlists, provide Recently Played and Continue Listening, and operate, secure and maintain the app.

PRAVOX also uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to support advertising delivery and consent management where required by applicable law and platform requirements.

4. Firebase

PRAVOX currently uses Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, to provide account and application functionality.

Firebase Storage and Cloud Functions may exist in project infrastructure, but this policy does not make broad claims about all user content stored by those services unless it is clearly supported by the current app behavior.

Firebase is a Google service and processing by Google is subject to Google’s applicable privacy practices.

5. YouTube API Services

PRAVOX uses YouTube API Services. PRAVOX uses YouTube services for music discovery and search, video metadata, and embedded YouTube playback.

PRAVOX may display information supplied by YouTube such as video titles, channel or artist information, thumbnails, and video identifiers.

The current PRAVOX search and playback flow does not download YouTube audio or video files or store downloaded YouTube media files in Firebase.

6. Data Sharing / Third-Party Services

PRAVOX relies on third-party service providers necessary to operate features, including relevant Google, Firebase and YouTube services. Information may therefore be processed by those services as necessary to provide their functionality and according to their applicable policies.

This policy does not identify any data brokers, advertisers, or analytics partners beyond the service providers required to operate the current app.

7. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

PRAVOX uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements in the app. Google and its advertising partners may process information associated with ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and consent choices, subject to Google’s policies and the user’s applicable consent choices.

Depending on region and consent status, advertising may be personalized or non-personalized where supported and applicable. PRAVOX uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage consent where required.

Where privacy options are required, users can access Privacy Choices from PRAVOX Account Settings to review or change applicable consent-related choices.

8. Data Control

Users can manage relevant data through available app controls, including Edit Profile, Clear Listening History, and Delete Account.

Clear Listening History removes the user’s Recently Played and Continue Listening history according to the current app implementation.

Account deletion removes the user’s PRAVOX account and associated PRAVOX user data according to the implemented deletion flow. PRAVOX does not guarantee deletion beyond the scope of that current implementation.

9. Data Security

PRAVOX uses reasonable technical measures and the security mechanisms provided by its service infrastructure to protect information. No system can guarantee absolute security against all risks.

10. Data Retention

Account-related information is retained as needed to provide the relevant PRAVOX functionality, subject to deletion controls and applicable service or legal requirements.

11. Children / Age

PRAVOX should be used in accordance with applicable age requirements and the requirements of third-party services used by the app. This policy does not set a specific minimum age without a verified owner-approved policy.

12. Changes to Privacy Policy

PRAVOX may update this Privacy Policy when functionality, services, or legal requirements change. Material changes should be reflected in the policy and effective date.
