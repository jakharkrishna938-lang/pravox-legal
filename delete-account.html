Account Support

Delete Your PRAVOX Account

App

PRAVOX

Developer/operator: PRAVOX

Method 1 — In app

Open PRAVOX, then go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. Follow the confirmation flow in the app to complete the deletion request process.

The current app implementation is designed to delete the signed-in user’s PRAVOX account and associated PRAVOX user data according to the active deletion flow.

Method 2 — External request

If you cannot access the app or account, you may request account deletion by contacting PRAVOX at jakharkrishna938@gmail.com.

Suggested subject: PRAVOX Account Deletion Request

Additional reasonable account verification may be needed before processing a request. Please do not send passwords. PRAVOX will only use the information needed to verify and process the account request.

What data is deleted

Based on the current app implementation, deletion is intended to remove the user’s PRAVOX account and associated PRAVOX user data handled by the implemented deletion flow. This may include account/profile information and personal library data such as Liked Songs, Favourite Songs, Playlists, and Recently Played / Continue Listening.

This page does not claim deletion of information controlled independently by Google, YouTube, or other third-party services. PRAVOX does not provide a deletion guarantee beyond the scope of the current account removal implementation.

Security notice

This website is informational and support-oriented only. It does not directly delete Firebase accounts from an email address or any browser form. No Firebase Admin credentials, service-account files, private keys, or API secrets are exposed on this site.
